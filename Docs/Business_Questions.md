# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Business Questions

The HR Analytics project aims to answer the following business questions:

### Employee Attrition

1. What is the overall employee attrition rate?

### Department Analysis

2. Which departments have the highest employee attrition?

### Job Role Analysis

3. Which job roles experience the highest employee turnover?

### Overtime Analysis

4. Does overtime influence employee attrition?

### Compensation Analysis

5. Does compensation appear to affect employee retention?

### Age Analysis

6. Which age groups experience higher employee attrition?

### Business Travel Analysis

7. Does business travel influence employee turnover?

### Education Analysis

8. Which education fields have higher employee attrition?

### Marital Status Analysis

9. Does marital status show differences in employee attrition?

### Work-Life Balance Analysis

10. How does work-life balance relate to employee retention?

### Tenure Analysis

11. Which job roles have the longest employee tenure?

### Retention Strategy

12. What strategies can organizations use to improve employee retention?

---

## Expected Business Outcome

The answers to these questions will help identify important patterns associated with employee attrition and support data-driven recommendations for improving employee retention and workforce planning.
